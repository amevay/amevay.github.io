What weather should we expect?

UPDATE: as of 11/14, the weather seems like it will be at the colder end of the normal range.

At the end of November, Palm Springs Weather is typically pleasant, with mild daytime temperatures and cooler nights. Daytime temperatures usually range from 70-80F. Evenings can drop to around 50-60F. Expect clear skies and no rain.

Are cell phones permitted at Sparrows?

Yes, however, the Bride and Groom request that, aside from taking photos and listening to music via headphones, all device use is kept to your room. Phones, laptops, tablets, etc., are not appropriate in the common areas of Sparrows, such as the courtyards, den, pool area, bar, dining areas, orchard, etc. Please bring a book (e-readers OK), a smile, and leave discussions of work and politics at home. ^

What's cool in Palm Springs?

Though Palm Springs is known for many attractions like the Aerial Tramway to Mt. San Jacinto, mid-century architecture, golf, botanic gardens, etc., we mostly enjoy it as a relaxing respite on the way between home and the Groom's office. Try strolling the main drag (Palm Canyon Drive), people watching, window shopping, and visiting some of our favorite restaurants:

  • Eight4Nine Restaurant & Lounge - Great for a boozy lunch. We spotted a T-Rex here once.
  • Las Casuelas Terraza - Unpretentious Mexican food in a beautiful open-air setting.
  • Tac/Quila - Pretentious Mexican food with patio and indoor seating.
  • Cartel Coffee - Best coffee in Palm Springs.
  • Cafe La Jefa - Good coffee, great indoor/outdoor location.
For a little extra excercise, borrow a bike from Sparrows to get there. BYO lights for the way home. ^
